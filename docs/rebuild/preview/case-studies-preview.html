Case study · Mobile + AI · 2026

Notebound.

An iOS app that turns a trip’s photos, notes, and voice into a written story and a mapped, day-by-day itinerary — powered by a multi-model Claude pipeline, built offline-first.

Design.

Travel is messy and hands-busy, so capture had to be effortless: hold to speak, or jot a line, tag it, and move on. The aesthetic is warm and quiet — the photos carry the color, the UI gets out of the way.

Features.

From a spoken note to a published story.

Voice → intent capture

Hold to record. A transcript is classified by a Claude Haiku function into the right kind of note and auto-tagged — food, stay, activity — with location attached.

Multimodal photo curation

Claude Opus actually looks at the photos, judges the strongest few per note, and picks a cover.

Auto-itinerary with map

The same call emits a structured day-by-day itinerary with coordinates.

Offline-first capture & sync

Notes and photos capture with no signal and drain through queues when connectivity returns.

EXIF & trip-aware location

Dates come from photo EXIF; a GPS fix is validated against the trip’s anchors.

Readiness gates

A local gate and a server-side LLM gate return an honest “not enough notes” state.

Architecture.

One generation call, the right model for each job. A React Native client talks to Supabase. Three Deno edge functions front Claude.

Model selection

Haiku keeps per-note cost near zero; Opus is reserved for the one call that warrants it.

Vision without blowing the budget

Photos downsized to ~1536px for the model; the blog embeds the originals.

Supplementary, never fatal

A malformed itinerary stores null but the narrative still saves.

Reliability backstop

pg_cron marks any run stuck past 5 minutes as errored.

Case study · Agentic AI · 2026

LaCuriosity.

A fully automated editorial platform publishing daily long-form articles across five thematic streams — Science, Psychology, Technology, Mathematics, and Ecology — each driven by a specialized Claude instance with its own persona, research mandate, and visual identity.

The pipeline handles everything without human intervention: demand-signal research via Tavily and Reddit, multi-agent drafting and fact-checking, Risograph-style image generation with Gemini, voiceover via ElevenLabs, video assembly via Creatomate, and distribution through Ghost CMS, email, and social. One daily trigger. Fifty-five nodes. Zero human touchpoints.
